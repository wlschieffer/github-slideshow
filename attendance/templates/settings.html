Today's schedule

Auto follows the weekday map below. Force pins a specific schedule for a special day (e.g. Pep Rally). Off turns off automatic period detection so staff choose the period on the kiosk — for non-traditional days. Remember to set this back to Auto afterward.

Scanner

Cooldown ignores a repeat read of the same ID within that many milliseconds, so a badge that scans twice in one swipe counts once (default 1500; 0 disables).

Passing-period lead is how many minutes before a period a scan counts for that period — set it to your passing-period length (default 7). So students scanning in the hallway before class (including before 1st period) are marked for the upcoming class, not the one that just ended.

Staff PIN

Protects the staff pages (Live, Reports, Roster, Schedule, Settings). The kiosk and scanning stay open for students. Default is 1234 — change it here.
